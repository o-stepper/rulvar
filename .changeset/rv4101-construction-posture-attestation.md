The profile hash sees the constructions, and counts what it cannot (RV4101; the debt RV4009 named). The regulated floor binds what flows through options, but the postures that decide whether a tool list can drift beneath a run (an `mcp()` source's `drift` and discovery bounds, RV1516/RV1808) or whether a provider executes tools outside the permission chain (the AI SDK bridge's `providerExecutedTools` seam) live on CONSTRUCTIONS the options never see; RV4009 excluded them from the hash by principle ("a hash must not imply what it cannot verify") and named them in prose. This train makes the verifiable part verified.
